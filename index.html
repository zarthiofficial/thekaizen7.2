About Kaizen

A Quarter's Worth of Milestones,
Celebrated Together

Kaizen is Zarthi's flagship quarterly event that brings together employees from across all Hives to celebrate achievements, share insights, strengthen connections, and align for the journey ahead. This quarter, we gather in Bhopal — embracing the rich culture and heritage of Madhya Pradesh for an evening where tradition, collaboration, and celebration meet.

Getting There

Let's Make The Journey a Part of The Story

Kaizen isn't just about the destination—it's about the memories we create along the way. For our Noida & Meerut Hives, we're planning a group train journey where we can travel together in our Zarthi T-shirts, play games, click pictures, make reels, and start celebrating long before we reach Bhopal. We'll also help coordinate your stay so you can room with your teammates.

We'll be happy to assist with train tickets and accommodation bookings, while travel and stay will be self-funded (or adjusted through salary deduction, where applicable). Travelling from Bangalore or Trivandrum? You're welcome to book your travel independently, as the distance and travel preferences vary. If you need any assistance with planning or accommodation, we're always happy to help.
